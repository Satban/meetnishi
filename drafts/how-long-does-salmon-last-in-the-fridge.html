How Long Does Salmon Last in the Fridge

By the Nishi team
Notes on food, your kitchen, and feeling good.

Share:

Salmon lasts about 1-2 days in the fridge after cooking and 1-2 days for raw salmon. Here’s how to store it safely.

Key Takeaways

  • Raw salmon lasts 1-2 days, cooked lasts 3-4 days.
  • Store salmon in an airtight container at 40°F or below.
  • Watch for strong odors, discoloration, or a slimy texture.
  • Freeze unused salmon to extend its lifespan.

How Long Does Salmon Last in the Fridge?

Fresh salmon usually lasts about 1 to 2 days in the fridge. If it's cooked, you can keep it for up to 3 to 4 days. But these timeframes can depend on how well you store it, so let's get into the details!

The Details

For raw salmon, try to use it within 1-2 days after purchasing it. If you’re not planning to cook it right away, consider freezing it to extend its shelf life. Cooked salmon can hold up longer and is generally safe for about 3-4 days, as long as it’s properly stored in an airtight container at a consistent refrigerator temperature of 40°F (4°C) or below.

If you bought vacuum-sealed salmon, it might last a bit longer, but it's best to stick to those 1-2 days for raw salmon, just to be on the safe side. If you ever find salmon on sale, it might be tempting to buy in bulk. Just remember to freeze what you won’t use right away.

The Science

So, why do these timeframes matter? Salmon, like other seafood, is highly perishable due to its moisture content and protein structure. Bacteria and enzymes can lead to spoilage, and when salmon is kept in the danger zone (40-140°F), the risk of bacterial growth increases. Pathogens like Salmonella and Listeria can thrive if the salmon isn't stored properly.

Signs It's Gone Bad

How do you know when salmon has gone bad? Look out for:

  • A strong, fishy odor
  • Discoloration or a dull color
  • A slimy or tacky texture

If you notice any of these signs, it's a good idea to err on the side of caution and discard it.

Quick Reference

  • Raw Salmon: 1-2 days in the fridge
  • Cooked Salmon: 3-4 days in the fridge
  • Freezing Raw Salmon: Up to 6 months
  • Freezing Cooked Salmon: 2-3 months
