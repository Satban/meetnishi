How it works

How Nishi gets to know you.

Most AI tools open with a blank prompt. Nishi opens with something it noticed. The home surface is the product.

Day 1

Start with one thing.

Onboarding is three screens, about 45 seconds. We don't ask anything we can pull from Apple Health or your account.

Screen 1

Hello

Brand, one CTA. No swipeable feature carousel. No fake value props before you've felt anything.

Screen 2

Apple Health

One permission unlocks weeks of biometric history, your date of birth, and biological sex. Skippable, but most people say yes.

Screen 3

Sign in

Apple, Google, or email. Name and email come from there. No password to create.

Day 2 and after

The observation loop.

Nishi notices something specific about you, suggests an action, and follows up on the outcome. That's the loop.

It catches the patterns you'd miss.
