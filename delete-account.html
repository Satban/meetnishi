Delete your Nishi account

You can permanently delete your Nishi account and the data we hold on you at any time. Here's how: from inside the app, or by emailing us if you can't reach the app.

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Delete from inside the app

The fastest path is from inside Nishi. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open the Nishi app on your phone (iOS or Android).
  2. Tap the menu icon in the top-left of any tab to open settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete in the confirmation dialog.

You'll be signed out immediately and your account and data will be removed from Nishi's systems within 30 days. There is no undo.

Delete by email

If you can't open the app (for example, if your phone is lost or you've already uninstalled), email us and we'll process the deletion manually.

Email: support@meetnishi.com

Subject: Delete my Nishi account

Include: the email address you used to sign in to Nishi (Apple or Google sign-in). We use this to find your account.

We process email deletion requests within 7 business days. We may reply to confirm your identity before completing the deletion.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we remove the following from Nishi's systems:

  • Account information: email, display name, sign-in identifiers.
  • Health data: readings Nishi pulled from Apple Health or Health Connect (steps, heart rate, sleep, weight, exercise, and related categories) that are stored in your Nishi profile.
  • Kitchen + food data: receipts you scanned, items in your pantry, meal plans, saved recipes.
  • Chat history: your conversations with Nishi, including text and voice transcripts.
  • Photos you shared: any images you uploaded in chat (meal photos, label scans, etc.).
  • Voice recordings: audio captured by the voice feature.
  • Observations + memories: patterns Nishi noticed about you.
  • Subscription state: your Nishi-side subscription record (see below for App Store and Google Play).

What we keep, and why

A small amount of information may be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons:

  • Anonymized service logs: request and error logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes. These do not identify you after account deletion.
  • Tax + billing records: required by law to be kept by us and our payment processor (Apple, Google, or RevenueCat) for the period required by applicable tax authorities.

Subscriptions outside Nishi

Deleting your Nishi account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. To stop future charges, cancel through your platform:

  • iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Nishi → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nishi → Cancel.
