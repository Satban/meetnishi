How Long Does Cooked Pork Last?

Cooked pork will generally last about 3 to 4 days when stored in the refrigerator. If you opt to freeze it, you can keep it for about 2 to 3 months without losing much quality. So, if you find yourself with leftovers after a tasty dinner, you can feel good about storing them for a little while!

The Details

When it comes to storing cooked pork, make sure it's in an airtight container to keep it fresher for longer. If you plan to freeze it, portion it out into smaller servings. This way, you can defrost only what you need, which is always a handy tip to minimize food staying in the freezer too long.

The Science

Understanding why food spoils is helpful. Cooked pork, like any other meat, can be susceptible to bacteria growth, especially in the temperature danger zone (40-140°F). Bacteria like Salmonella and Listeria can develop if the pork is left out too long, which is why proper refrigeration is key.

Signs It's Gone Bad

If you're unsure about the freshness of your cooked pork, trust your senses. Look for changes in color, smell, and texture. If the pork has developed a sour smell or a slimy texture, it's time to let it go. Better safe than sorry!

Quick Reference

  • Freezer: 2 to 3 months
