    Your first week with Nishi

    A glimpse of the rhythm once the app is in your hands.

    • Day 1
      You connect Apple Health (or Health Connect on Android) and Nishi starts noticing what your day already looks like.
    • Day 2
      First observation: something specific about you that Nishi picked up from your sleep, your activity, and what you ate yesterday.
    • Day 3–4
      Patterns start forming. Nishi suggests a meal using what's in your kitchen and how you've been feeling.
    • Day 7
      Your first weekly recap, ready to share with your doctor or just keep for yourself. This is what Premium quietly produces in the background.
