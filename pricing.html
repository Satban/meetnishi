Common questions

About pricing.

Is there really a free trial?

Yes. 7 days of full Premium with no card. After day 7 you fall to the free tier. No surprise charge, no data loss.

What happens after the trial?

If you do nothing, you keep using Nishi on the free tier. Free includes daily observations, basic chat, and food logging. It never expires. Premium features go quieter; nothing breaks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open Settings, tap cancel, you're done. Your free tier continues automatically. No retention call.

Do you take HSA or FSA?

In a future release we'll surface HSA/FSA receipts via a partner (Truemed or Flex). Until then, your subscription receipt is itemized clearly enough that many users get reimbursed.

How is this different from Noom or MyFitnessPal?

Noom is behavior coaching with lessons. MyFitnessPal is logging. Nishi is interpretation. It notices patterns across your food, your wearables, and how you feel, then acts on what it sees. Built for the GLP-1 reality, not the calorie-counting era.

Do I need to be on a GLP-1 medication to use Nishi?

No. Nishi is built for the GLP-1 reality but works for anyone navigating nutrition with intention. The Premium features around CGM, doctor summary, and medication-aware suggestions are most valuable to people on GLP-1.

Is my health data private?

Your individual data is never sold. We don't have advertising relationships with any healthcare company. Our messaging principles and data handling are described in detail on the Story page.

What if I subscribe on iOS and on the web?

One account, one entitlement. Whichever surface you subscribe on syncs to all your devices. We recommend the web subscription. It's the same price with no Apple or Google fee.
