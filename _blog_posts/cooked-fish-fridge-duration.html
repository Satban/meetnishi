How Long Does Cooked Fish Last in the Fridge?

Cooked fish can last for about 3 to 4 days in the fridge when stored correctly. Ensuring your fish is kept in an airtight container can help maintain its freshness for as long as possible.

The Details

When storing cooked fish, it's best to let it cool to room temperature before sealing it up in an airtight container or wrapping it tightly with plastic wrap or aluminum foil. Storing it in the coldest part of your fridge, typically at a temperature of 32°F to 39°F, will also help keep it safe.

Remember, different types of fish might have slight variations in shelf life, but the 3 to 4 days guideline generally applies. For instance, salmon may hold up a bit longer, while more delicate fish like flounder might not. Always try to label your container with the date you cooked the fish for easy tracking!

The Science

Why does cooked fish last only a few days? Well, it’s all about bacteria, enzymes, and oxidation. Cooked fish can become a breeding ground for harmful bacteria like Salmonella or Listeria if not stored properly. The USDA advises that fish should be kept at temperatures below 40°F to avoid the so-called “danger zone” (40°F - 140°F) where bacteria can thrive.

Signs It's Gone Bad

Knowing when your fish has gone bad is important. If you see a dull appearance, if it smells sour, or if it feels slimy to the touch, it’s best to throw it away. Fresh fish generally has a mild aroma, and when cooked, it should maintain a pleasant scent.

Quick Reference

  • Cooked fish lasts: 3 to 4 days in the fridge.
  • Store in an airtight container.
  • Ensure fridge temperature is at or below 40°F.
  • Look for signs like sour smell or slimy texture before eating.
