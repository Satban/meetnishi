How it works

What your organization does, and what participants experience.

No integration and no member lists. You see totals; personal details stay between each participant and Nishi.

    Your organization

    Plan your program

    Participants

Questions

Good to know.

Who pays?

Your organization pays for the seats. The people you offer it to pay nothing.

What do people get?

Nishi Premium, the same app someone would buy for themselves, for as long as your plan runs.

What can we see?

Totals only: how many people joined and how many seats are in use. We show these once 10 or more people have joined. We never share names, emails or anything a person logs.

How do people join?

You share a private link we set up for you. People open it, sign in with Apple or Google, and Premium switches on. There is nothing to integrate and no list to send us.

How long is the agreement?

One year. You can add seats during the year, and we send the renewal invoice 30 days before it ends.

Is Nishi a medical service?

No. Nishi is a food and wellness companion. It does not give medical advice, and it works alongside the care people already get.
