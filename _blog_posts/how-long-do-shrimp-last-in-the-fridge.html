How Long Do Shrimp Last in the Fridge?

Shrimp typically lasts about 1-2 days in the fridge after cooking and up to 2-3 days when they are raw. Proper storage is key to keeping them fresh and safe to eat.

The Details

When you bring home shrimp, plan to use them quickly. If they’re fresh and raw, aim to cook them within 2-3 days. If you cooked them, you should consume them within 1-2 days to ensure they remain tasty and safe. Store them in an airtight container or tightly wrapped in plastic wrap to maintain freshness and limit exposure to air.

The Science

Shrimp, like all seafood, is prone to spoilage due to bacteria and enzymes. The USDA notes that refrigeration slows down the growth of pathogenic bacteria, which can thrive in temperatures above 40°F. If left out at temperatures in the danger zone (between 40°F and 140°F), shrimp can spoil more quickly. Bacteria like Salmonella and Listeria can develop if shrimp are not stored properly, so keeping track of the time they’ve been in your fridge helps keep you safe.

Signs It's Gone Bad

Before cooking or eating shrimp, check for these indicators of spoilage: a strong, fishy odor; slimy texture; or a change in color, such as turning gray. If any of these signs are present, it's best to err on the side of caution and discard them.

Quick Reference

  • Raw shrimp: 2-3 days in the fridge
  • Cooked shrimp: 1-2 days in the fridge
  • Storage: Use airtight containers or wrap tightly
  • Check for odor, texture, and color changes

FAQ

Q: Can you freeze shrimp to make it last longer?
A: Yes, shrimp can be frozen for up to six months. Just make sure to store them in an airtight container to prevent freezer burn.

Q: How can I tell if shrimp is fresh?
A: Fresh shrimp should have a mild, seawater-like aroma, firm texture, and a translucent appearance. Avoid any with strong odors or discoloration.
