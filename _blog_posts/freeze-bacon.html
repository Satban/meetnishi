Can You Freeze Bacon?

Yes, you can freeze bacon! Freezing is a great way to extend its shelf life while maintaining its taste and quality.

The Details

To freeze bacon, first, it’s best to keep it in its original packaging as long as you can. If you've opened it, simply wrap the bacon tightly in plastic wrap or aluminum foil, or place it in a freezer bag, making sure to remove as much air as possible. For best results, divide it into portions that reflect how much you typically use at a time. This way, you don’t have to thaw more than you need at once.

When properly stored, cooked bacon can be kept in the freezer for up to 2-3 months, and uncooked bacon can last about 6-8 months.

The Science

Freezing bacon stops the growth of bacteria, preventing spoilage. While bacteria such as Salmonella and Listeria can thrive at temperatures between 40°F and 140°F (the danger zone), freezing at 0°F halts their activity. Enzymatic reactions that can degrade the quality of food also slow down significantly in the freezer.

Signs It's Gone Bad

While frozen bacon does have a longer shelf life, it can still develop freezer burn if not wrapped properly, leading to dry or off flavors. If you notice discoloration or a dry, leathery texture, it’s a sign that your bacon may not taste as great as fresh. Always smell it after thawing; if it smells sour or off, it’s best to discard it.

Quick Reference

  • Freezing bacon is safe and effective.
  • Use air-tight wrapping to avoid freezer burn.
  • Cooked bacon lasts 2-3 months in the freezer; uncooked lasts 6-8 months.
  • Check for signs of freezer burn before use.
